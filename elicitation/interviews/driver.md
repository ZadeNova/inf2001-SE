**Driver Interview**

**Operational & Daily Routine Questions**

**1. Walk us through a typical working day, from receiving the schedule to finishing work.**

"I log into the web application on my phone or van tablet in the morning to view my assigned jobs for the day. I check the van number I’m assigned to, the assigned technicians, and the sequence of locations. After picking up the technicians and loading the necessary aircon units/parts, I drive from job to job. At each stop, I assist with unloading and wait while the technicians complete the service or installation. Once all jobs are completed, I drive back to headquarters and log off."

**2. What information do you need before setting off for a job?**

"I need the target customer address/location, customer contact number, assigned brand (M Electric or Dicon), time window, job type (installation or servicing), van license plate, and the names of the qualified technicians assigned to my van."

**3. What do you do while technicians are carrying out a job?**

"I remain on standby at or near the vehicle, assist with heavy lifting or tools if required, safeguard the remaining equipment in the van, and prepare for navigation to the next location."

**4. How should travel, waiting and other duties be reflected in your workload?**

"Travel time between jobs and waiting time on site are part of my active working hours. My total daily workload calculation shouldn't just be the time technicians spend installing an aircon—it must include estimated driving time between job locations so my total weekly workload accurately reflects actual hours and flags if I exceed 40 hours. We track this workload by correlating estimated route hours alongside the vehicle's daily mileage logs."

**Route, Scheduling & Contingency Questions**

**5. How are routes and the order of jobs decided?**

"The order of jobs is assigned by the manager during the weekly allocation. However, showing job locations and customer time windows on our schedule helps us follow an optimised route without driving back and forth across town."

**6. What happens when travel delays or overrunning jobs affect your schedule?**

"If a job runs over time or traffic delays us, it pushes back the rest of the day's schedule. I need a way to see real-time updates or inform the manager if a job will breach working hour limits or conflict with another appointment. When delays occur, the remaining jobs are typically pushed back to another day, or overtime (OT) is automatically flagged for approval if we must complete it that evening."

**7. How are changes to your van or crew handled during the day?**

"If a technician falls ill mid-day, the manager might reassign a replacement technician to my van. Any crew changes must adhere to the rule that the crew has the right certification for the job (M Electric or Dicon) and stays within the 2 to 3 person crew limit. The web app should update dynamically to reflect my new crew."

**8. What happens when your assigned van becomes unavailable?**

"Vans go for routine workshop servicing every 2 months or suffer unexpected breakdowns. If a van is unavailable, the manager reassigns us to an available standby van from the fleet of 6. At least 3 active vans must remain on the road daily."

**Availability, Preferences & System Interaction Questions**

**9. How would you record your availability and later changes?**

"I submit my availability manually on a paper form to the manager up to 5 weeks in advance. I know the hard deadline is Wednesday before Thursday's allocation planning. If I need to change availability after Wednesday, I must contact the manager for case-by-case approval directly via WhatsApp or email."

**10. What job preferences would you want the manager to consider?**

"I would like to indicate preferences such as preferred working days, preferred geographic sectors/locations, or preferred shift times. The system should display these preferences to the manager on the job allocation screen when they assign jobs."

**11. What would you do if you could not fulfil an assigned job?**

"Under our current manual process, I have to contact my boss immediately via a phone call. The manager then manually finds a replacement driver or reassigns the crew to another available van."

**12. How should assignment changes reach you?**

"Since we don't have an app, any last-minute assignment changes have to reach me through direct phone notifications like a call or a WhatsApp text from the manager."

**13. What information would help you understand your weekly assignments and monthly workload?**

"On my landing page, I need a visual display showing my daily job assignments for the week and a summary bar/chart showing my overall accumulated hours for the month. It should clearly show if I am approaching or exceeding my target hours."

**14. How would you access the system during your working day?**

"I access it via a mobile-responsive web app on a smartphone or tablet while in the van or at headquarters."

**15. What important issue have we not covered?**

"We haven't explicitly covered how the system handles annual leave tracking. Since all 17 staff members have 7 days of annual leave, the system needs to prevent us from being assigned to a van roster if we are on approved leave, especially since we must maintain at least 3 active vans daily."

**16. Which of the needs discussed matters most to you, and why?**

"The 40-hour weekly workload cap tracking matters most. Balancing work-life balance is a major goal for the company, so having a visual warning when we approach or exceed 40 hours helps prevent burnout and keeps the scheduling fair."

**17. What have we misunderstood in our summary of your answers?**

"The initial requirements state that we can reject jobs but will be warned to discuss them with the manager first. My responses should align with this system warning feature rather than assuming the manager handles every single rejection completely outside of the platform."
