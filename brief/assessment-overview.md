## AI Usage

While it is not prohibited to use AI for the team project, all teams must use AI tools critically and ensure the learning outcomes are achieved.

In case AI tools are used, please include a section at the beginning of the Milestone Report and articulate (i) how and where AI tools are used; and (ii) what changes are made on top of AI outputs.

Please note all teams will be asked to elaborate on randoms parts during presentations.

## Appendix A - Instructions to Engage Stakeholders for Team Project

### As the Project Team

Here's a quick summary of the minimal duty required for each individual team.

1. You may find team members in your own group or from another group or from the real industry to be the Stakeholder Representatives for your team project.
2. Every team should have **engage at least 5 Stakeholder Representatives with at most 2 representatives from your own team.**
3. Try to arrange in-person meetings with the Stakeholder Representatives **as early as possible** from Week 2 of the trimester. It is the team's responsibility to initiate the meeting and keep your Stakeholder Representatives familiarized with the project context and requirements in advance such that they're able to provide valuable inputs.

### If you're approached by other teams to act as their Stakeholder Representative

1. Familiarise yourself with the project description and requirements in advance. In the project description, there are some pointers for the stakeholders to consider.
2. Consider possible gaps that the team may question. You will want to think of an answer or process so that you can give the project team a uniform answer.
3. Please provide all your best assistance to play the role of the Stakeholder Representatives. Each student **cannot be acting as Stakeholder Representatives for more than two teams** (including your own team) and cannot be engaged in Stakeholder meetings for the same project.
4. Please keep your engagement discussions with other teams confidentially.

### During the Engagement Meeting with Stakeholders

1. The students may use various requirement gathering technique during the Stakeholder's meeting, and the Stakeholder Representatives should work with them during the meeting.
2. The Stakeholder Representative is allowed to add requirements as they deem fit. However, the requirements in the project description are the minimum each project team must deliver.
3. Record the attendance of the meeting and include it in your Milestone Report.

### Deadlines

1. Arrangement of the Stakeholders meeting can only start from Week 2 of the trimester.
2. All engagements (in-person, email, virtually) must end by Week 6 of the trimester.
